Leg capture (sample data)

Records the headset camera plus head and hand poses while a voice-free, beep-guided script asks for seated leg motions (about 7 minutes). Everything stays on this headset until you download it. Sit on a chair with both feet on the floor and some light in the room.

  1. Press 1. Allow camera and accept the camera prompt.
  2. Pick a camera; check the preview shows something when you look down at your knees.
  3. Press 2. Start capture. Follow the panel and the beeps. One beep = one repetition.
  4. When the panel says "Done", VR ends by itself. Press both download links. To record again, reload the page.
